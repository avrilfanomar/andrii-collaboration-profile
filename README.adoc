= Collaboration Profile
:toc: macro

A realistic professional collaboration profile system to set expectations, assess fit, and anticipate friction points.

toc::[]

== What is this?

This project provides a structured way to document and visualize how you work with others. Unlike a marketing bio or resume, it focuses on *collaboration realities*: your preferences, boundaries, working style, values, and both strengths and gaps.

The result is an interactive visualization that helps teams, managers, and collaborators understand who you are and how best to work with you.

== How it works

The system has two main parts:

. *`collaboration_profile_graph.json`* — Structured data about you
** Your values, principles, and boundaries
** Working preferences and behaviors
** Skills, interests, and development areas
** Examples and evidence from your experience
** Interpretations and patterns

. *`index.html`* — Interactive visualization
** Browse by category (guidance, values, skills, etc.)
** Filter by evidence type (self-reported, CV claims, examples, hypotheses)
** Search and explore the full profile
** Respects light/dark mode preferences

== How to build your own

=== Quick start

. Clone or fork this repository
. Edit `collaboration_profile_graph.json` with your own information
. Open `index.html` in a browser to see your profile
. Share the HTML file or host it anywhere

=== Using an LLM to build your profile

The easiest way to create your profile is through a structured conversation with an LLM (like Claude):

. Have the LLM interview you about your working style
. Ask it to generate the JSON following the schema in this repository
. Review and refine the generated profile
. Use the included HTML viewer to visualize it

The schema is self-documenting — check `collaboration_profile_graph.json` for the structure and metadata fields.

=== What to include

Focus on information that helps others collaborate with you effectively:

* *Do's and Don'ts* — Clear guidance for working together
* *Values & Boundaries* — What matters most, what's non-negotiable
* *Working Style* — Preferences, practices, communication patterns
* *Skills & Gaps* — Honest assessment of strengths and development areas
* *Examples* — Concrete evidence from your experience
* *Interpretations* — Patterns and insights about how you work

== Why this matters

Traditional profiles focus on what you've done. Collaboration profiles focus on *how you work*.

This helps:

* Teams assess cultural and working-style fit
* Managers understand how to support you
* Collaborators anticipate potential friction
* You reflect on your own patterns and preferences
